Fractional Technology & AI Leadership

Most firms know they need to move on AI.
Almost none need a full-time technology executive to do it.

Execution can be taught. Direction cannot be improvised.

The tools are increasingly accessible, and existing employees can be trained to use them well. What is missing in most mid-sized firms is the layer above the tools — someone accountable for deciding where to invest, what to build versus buy, how to sequence it, and how to govern it once it is live. That role is real work, but it rarely justifies a $300–400K hire.

Robertson Consulting holds that seat on a fractional basis.
